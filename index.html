About Me

Hi, I'm Joshua

I am an Astrophysical and Planetary Sciences student at the University of Colorado Boulder, and a graduate of Austin Community College.

As a student, I've been involved in various research from Protoplanetary Disk Spectroscopy to Computational Aeronomy, and acted as a Science Policy Advocate speaking directly to Congressional Representatives in support of science.

You can find my CV here. (Last updated 8/11/2026)

Research

Currently, I am working under Dr. Dolon Bhattacharyya and Dr. Kevin France at the Laboratory for Atmospheric and Space Physics (LASP) on a near-ultraviolet project with archival NASA CUTE observations of solar system targets.

I am also an alumni of the Boulder Solar Alliance NSF REU, where I worked under Dr. Katelynn Greer in an Observing System Simulation Experiment (OSSE) to evaluate simulated satellite observations of Joule Heating in VLEO.

Previous work includes Protoplanetary Disk spectroscopy in infrared under Dr. Andrea Banzatti where I had 12 night observations with the NASA IRTF iSHELL telescope, and even Environmental Science Soil surveys of carbon respiration in varied agricultural methods under Dr. Jenny Cooke.

Advocacy and outreach

I am a two-time Student Ambassador for the American Physical Society, and through that position I've had the opportunity to advocate for science policy directly on The Hill in Washington DC.

In addition, I volunteer frequently with Astronomy on Tap and have developed educational physics media for the Contemporary Physics Education Project (CPEP) that was shared on all company pages.
